## Tailored Coaching Sessions

Sessions are structured around what you want to achieve, whether at a local indoor climbing wall or making the transition to natural rock.

Common areas of focus include:

* **Indoor Lead Climbing:** Building solid clips, belay confidence, and effective lead tactics.
* **Transitioning Outdoors:** Translating indoor skills to natural crags, reading rock, and adapting technique.
* **Movement & Technique:** Footwork accuracy, body position, steep ground technique, and dynamic movement.
* **Tactics & Efficiency:** Route reading, pacing, resting strategies, and energy conservation.
* **Mindset & Confidence:** Fall practice, managing fear of falling, and building mental resilience.

## Who Is It For?

Private coaching is available for:

* **Individual Climbers:** Focused 1-to-1 coaching tailored to your exact pace and objectives.
* **Pairs & Small Groups:** Shared sessions for climbing partners working on similar goals.
* **All Ability Levels:** From relative beginners wanting solid fundamentals to experienced climbers breaking into new grades or environments.
